Grill Pro terms

Short and plain. Last updated 27 September 2026.

Who we are

Grill is made by Hold. You can reach us at support@grillyour.ai.

What you get

A starter key is free. No card. It includes $0.50 of judge spend in total, once, for one verified email. It does not refill. One starter key per email. Bring your own key and checks stay free and unlimited on your own credit, with no account.

Grill Pro, when paid plans are on, gives you a personal key for Grill’s one-click check in Claude Desktop and Claude Code. Each paid key includes up to $3 of AI time a month, and a typical check costs about a cent. That allowance resets each month and doesn’t roll over.

Price and cancelling

Grill Pro is $9 a month or $90 a year, billed through Stripe, plus any sales tax. If the site is offering early access when you subscribe, that price is half off for as long as you keep the subscription. You can cancel anytime from your subscription page. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Refunds

If Grill Pro isn’t for you, email us within 30 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it.

Your Pro account

An account is only for a key we manage, including a free starter key. You sign in with a link we email you. There is no password. We keep your email, whether a paid subscription is on, which assistant and judge you picked, and a hash of your key. We don’t keep the key itself. Bring your own key and there is no account.

Your decisions

By default Grill stores none of what you decide. Your write-ups go from your assistant straight to the AI services that check them, which don’t keep them. Grill does not email you a verdict or a weekly review. On an account you can turn on saving copies of reports. Saving is off until you do. You can delete any copy, or all of them, and you can turn saving off. We never put that text on your Stripe customer. We count sign-ups, keys issued, first grills, allowances used up and upgrade clicks. Never the text of a decision. The free tool has no account and never stores a write-up. The privacy notes have the details.

Fair use

Your key is for you. Please don’t share or resell it. One starter key per verified email. If a key is being misused, we may switch it off and refund the rest of a paid period.

What Grill is, and isn’t

Grill gives you a second opinion from an AI, and AI can be wrong. Your decisions stay yours. Grill isn’t legal, financial, medical or tax advice.

Limits

Grill is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you’ve paid us in the last 12 months.

Changes

If these terms change, we’ll update this page and email subscribers before the change affects them.
