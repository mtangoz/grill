Grill terms

Short and plain. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Who we are

Grill is made by Hold Systems, a DBA, the trading name of a sole proprietor. Postal address: [address coming soon]. You can reach us at support@grillyour.ai. How we handle your data is on the privacy page.

What Grill is, and isn’t

Grill gives you a second opinion from an AI, and AI can be wrong. Your decisions stay yours: Grill advises, you decide, and Grill never acts on a verdict. Grill isn’t legal, financial, medical or tax advice.

The free tool

The installed tool, the skill and the copy-and-paste prompt are free and open source under the MIT licence. They run with your own model-router key, or in the AI you paste into. There is no account and Grill keeps nothing.

Grill inside your chat

The in-chat connector (grillyour.ai/mcp) needs a sign-in with a verified email. While it is invite-only, only invited emails can grill. Each person gets a small free starter credit, once, that does not refill. One starter credit per person: an agent signed in for you shares yours.

Free use has limits: a daily number of grills per account, and monthly spending limits per person and for the whole service. When a limit is reached, the grill doesn’t run and you’re told in plain words. Nothing is charged to you. We may change these limits, and we’ll keep the current ones in the public docs.

Your decision passes through Grill’s server in memory and is never logged or stored. Decision records are kept only if you say yes, and you can export or delete them, or delete the whole account, at any time from your account page.

Using Grill fairly

Please don’t use Grill to break the law, to harm or harass anyone, to try to get around the limits (for example with many sign-ins), or to overload or attack the service. Don’t share or resell a key we manage for you. If an account or key is being misused, we may switch it off.

What you send

You keep the rights to what you write. You let us pass it to the AI services that check it, only to give you the grill. Don’t send other people’s private information, or passwords and keys (Grill stops the run if it spots one).

Grill Pro, when paid plans are on

A starter key is free. No card. It includes $0.50 of judge spend in total, once, for one verified email. It does not refill. One starter key per email. Bring your own key and checks stay free and unlimited on your own credit, with no account.

Grill Pro, when paid plans are on, gives you a personal key for Grill’s one-click check in Claude Desktop and Claude Code. Each paid key includes up to $3 of AI time a month, and a typical check costs about a cent. That allowance resets each month and doesn’t roll over.

Grill Pro is $9 a month or $90 a year, billed through Stripe, plus any sales tax. If the site is offering early access when you subscribe, that price is half off for as long as you keep the subscription. You can cancel anytime from your subscription page. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

If Grill Pro isn’t for you, email us within 30 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it.

A Pro account is only for a key we manage, including a free starter key. You sign in with a link we email you. There is no password. We keep your email, whether a paid subscription is on, which assistant and judge you picked, and a hash of your key. We don’t keep the key itself. On a Pro account you can turn on saving copies of reports. Saving is off until you do, and you can delete any copy. We never put that text on your Stripe customer.

Availability

Grill is provided as it is. We try to keep it running, but it can be slow or down, and the AI services it relies on can change. We may change or stop a feature; if we stop the in-chat connector, you’ll be able to export your records first.

Limits

As far as the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you’ve paid us in the last 12 months.

Changes

If these terms change, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters for paying subscribers, we’ll email them before it affects them.
