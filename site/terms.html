Grill terms

Short and plain. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Who we are

Grill is made by Hold Systems, a DBA, the trading name of a sole proprietor. Postal address: 418 Broadway # 12493, Albany, NY 12207, USA. You can reach us at support@grillyour.ai. How we handle your data is on the privacy page.

What Grill is, and isn’t

Grill gives you a second opinion from an AI, and AI can be wrong. Your decisions stay yours: Grill advises, you decide, and Grill never acts on a verdict. Grill isn’t legal, financial, medical or tax advice.

The free tool

The installed tool, the skill and the copy-and-paste prompt are free and open source under the MIT licence. They run with your own model-router key, or in the AI you paste into. There is no account. Grill keeps a grill from the free tool only when you send that one.

Grill inside your chat

The in-chat connector (grillyour.ai/mcp) needs a sign-in with a verified email. Anyone can sign up. Each person gets 100 free checks a month. One allowance per person: an agent signed in for you uses yours.

Free use has limits: a daily number of grills per account, and monthly spending limits per person and for the whole service. When a limit is reached, the grill doesn’t run and you’re told in plain words. Nothing is charged to you. We may change these limits, and we’ll keep the current ones in the public docs.

Only completed grills count as checks. Retries, errors and follow-ups like 'more' or 'options' don't.

Early testers invited to Grill in chat get 100 free checks a month, resets on the 1st (UTC). Unused free checks do not roll over. A usual grill uses 1 check. An unusually long one can use more. No card is needed for the free checks. Past that, extra checks are a prepaid pack bought on the account page: 100 checks for $5. Unused checks from a pack carry over. Automatic add is off unless you tick it. Nothing is charged on its own when it is off. If automatic add is on, another pack is charged to your saved card when you run out, up to your monthly ceiling. The ceiling starts at $20 a month. You can turn it off on your account page. We never put the words of a decision on a Stripe customer. If Grill’s free checks for everyone run out in a month, grills pause until the 1st, and nothing is charged to you.

Your decision passes through Grill’s server to the judge and is not logged, except that a /judge link carries it in its web address. The decision and the verdict are held encrypted for 15 minutes, records on or off. The decision is kept for 180 days only if you send that grill after a reaction. Decision records are kept only if you say yes, and you can export or delete them, or delete the whole account, at any time from your account page.

Using Grill fairly

Please don’t use Grill to break the law, to harm or harass anyone, to try to get around the limits (for example with many sign-ins), or to overload or attack the service. Don’t share or resell a key we manage for you. If an account or key is being misused, we may switch it off.

What you send

You keep the rights to what you write. You let us pass it to the AI services that check it, only to give you the grill. Don’t send other people’s private information, or passwords and keys (Grill stops the run if it spots one).

Availability

Grill is provided as it is. We try to keep it running, but it can be slow or down, and the AI services it relies on can change. We may change or stop a feature; if we stop the in-chat connector, you’ll be able to export your records first.

Limits

As far as the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you’ve paid us in the last 12 months.

Changes

If these terms change, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters for someone who pays for extra checks, we’ll email them before it affects them.
