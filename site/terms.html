Grill Pro terms

Short and plain. Last updated 26 September 2026.

Who we are

Grill is made by Hold. You can reach us at hello@hold.quest.

What you get

Grill Pro gives you a personal key for Grill’s one-click check in Claude Desktop and Claude Code. Each key includes up to $3 of AI time a month, and a typical check costs about a cent. The allowance resets each month and doesn’t roll over.

Price and cancelling

Grill Pro is $9 a month or $90 a year, billed through Stripe, plus any sales tax. If the site is offering early access when you subscribe, that price is half off for as long as you keep the subscription. You can cancel anytime from your subscription page. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Refunds

If Grill Pro isn’t for you, email us within 30 days of your first payment and we’ll refund it.

Your decisions

Grill never stores what you decide. Your write-ups go from your Claude straight to the AI services that check them, which don’t keep them. For Pro we keep only what billing needs: your email, your subscription, and your key’s usage (never the text of a check). The privacy notes have the details.

Fair use

Your key is for you. Please don’t share or resell it. If a key is being misused, we may switch it off and refund the rest of your period.

What Grill is, and isn’t

Grill gives you a second opinion from an AI, and AI can be wrong. Your decisions stay yours. Grill isn’t legal, financial, medical or tax advice.

Limits

Grill is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you’ve paid us in the last 12 months.

Changes

If these terms change, we’ll update this page and email subscribers before the change affects them.
