Look back on a decision.

For the copy-and-paste route. Turn the judge’s answer into a decision record, then come back and say what happened. Nothing is sent and nothing is stored.

What a record looks like

decisionTake the job in Denver. I’m 80% sure it’s right.

resultshaky · it assumes you’ll love living there, and nothing checks that

weak spot“we’ll figure out the city once we’re there”

quick checkspend a long weekend there and do the commute at rush hour

Before you decide

  1. What do you expect to happen, and by when? Write the prediction you will stand behind.
  2. How sure are you now, as a percent? A band is fine. This is your confidence, not the judge.
  3. What would prove you wrong? Name the one result you will treat as decisive.
```grill-record
version: 1
date: 2026-09-27
title: Take the job in Denver
prediction: I will still want the job after a month in Denver
verdict: shaky
falsifier: spend a long weekend there and do the commute at rush hour
confidence: 80%
review: 2026-10-11
```
