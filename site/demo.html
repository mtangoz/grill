See a grill before you set one up.

Four made-up decisions, in the same short format as a grill.

These are made-up decisions and illustrative verdicts, written to show the format, not real users’ decisions and not saved records.

YouGrill this: ChatGPT drafted my memo recommending we move customer support to a cheaper outside firm to save 30%. I’m sending it to my boss this afternoon. I’m 75% sure it’s right.

GrillShaky. It assumes the 30% saving survives handover and rework, and nothing in the memo checks that. Cheap test: ask the firm for a two-week paid pilot on one queue and compare cost per resolved ticket.Say ‘more’, ‘options’, or your call.

more

The strongest case against is a saving that disappears in rework and unhappy customers. A short pilot can check cost and resolution rates before you send it. Whether the risk to your team is worth it is your call.

YouGrill this: I’m buying the used car this weekend. I’m 70% sure it’s right.

GrillSolid, if… It assumes a mechanic has already checked the gearbox, and nothing checks that. Cheap test: book a pre-purchase inspection before you pay.Say ‘more’, ‘options’, or your call.

more

The strongest case against is a hidden repair that costs more than the car. A mechanic can check the gearbox and the service history. Whether you still want the car after that is your call.

YouGrill this: I’m signing a two-year lease on the smaller place. I’m 60% sure it’s right.

GrillShaky. It assumes you’ll stay for the full two years, and nothing checks that. Cheap test: price a one-year lease and write down what would make you move.Say ‘more’, ‘options’, or your call.

more

The strongest case against is being locked in if you need to leave early. A shorter term and the break fee can be checked with the landlord. Whether two years fits your life is your call.
