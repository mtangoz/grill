About Grill.

Hear the strongest case against your plan, then decide for yourself.

What Grill is

Grill your AI before you send it to your boss. Hear the strongest case against your plan, then decide for yourself. Get a second opinion before you decide. A model from another company gives a verdict and one cheap test. The judge never comes from the company behind your AI, so it won’t share its blind spots.

Grill helps people make better decisions with AI. An AI from a different company argues the other side; you decide; later you look back and see how your judgement did. The verdict never becomes the decision. The free tool keeps nothing; Grill in chat keeps only what you choose, encrypted, and you can delete it. You decide. Grill gives you the case against and a cheap test. The call stays yours. To keep a record and look back later, use the look-back page.

Free, with the AI you already use. See three more examples. The code is on GitHub. The plain terms are on Terms.

Privacy

Your decisions are never public. We never publish a decision, and we never tie one to your name in public. Not in examples, case studies, marketing or anywhere else. The free tool has no account and keeps none of your decisions. Every detail of what is sent and counted is in Privacy.
