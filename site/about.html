About Grill.

Hear the strongest case against your plan, then decide for yourself.

What Grill is

Grill your AI before you send it to your boss. A model from another company gives a verdict and one cheap test. The judge never comes from the company behind your AI, so it won’t share its blind spots.

An AI from a different company argues the other side. You decide. Later you can look back and see how your judgement did. The free tool keeps a grill only when you send that one; Grill in chat keeps only what you choose, encrypted, and you can delete it. If you use Grill in chat, those choices are at Your grills. The look-back page is the paste option when you are not using Grill in chat.

Free, with the AI you already use. See three more examples. The code is on GitHub. The plain terms are on Terms.

Who makes it

Grill in chat

Anyone can sign up and connect Grill to the AI they use at https://grillyour.ai/mcp. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste that address. Asking Claude in a chat to connect it does not work. The steps are at grillyour.ai/start/.

Privacy

Your decisions are never public. We never publish a decision, and we never tie one to your name in public. Not in examples, case studies, marketing or anywhere else. The free tool has no account and keeps none of your decisions. Every detail of what is sent and counted is in Privacy.
