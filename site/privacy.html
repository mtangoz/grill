Your privacy, in plain words.

The free tool has no account and keeps nothing. Here is everything that is sent, and everything that is counted.

Your decisions

The reflection at the end of a grill stays in your notes. This website does not store it. Only the short summary you approve is shared, never your notes or files. If you copy and paste, it goes to the AI you paste it into. With the one-click version, it goes only to AI services that don’t store it, plus a quality check that’s on by default (Grill tells you each time, and you can turn it off). Email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers are hidden before anything is sent. And Grill gets better by practicing on made-up examples, never on your decisions.

Accounts and keys

An account is only for a key we manage. A starter allowance needs no card. Bring your own key and there is no account. We see your email, the assistant and judge you pick, and your key’s usage (cost and model, never the text). We count sign-ups, keys issued, first grills, allowances used up and upgrade clicks. We don’t keep what you decide unless you turn on saving copies of reports on that account, and you can delete them.

This website and the installed tool

This website counts visits anonymously, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you. The installed tool can optionally send one anonymous usage ping to this site after the first successful grill, if you turn that on at install; off by default; never again; never decision words.

The same count can record that a decision record was shown or copied, that a look-back started or finished, and how many records were pasted. It can also record a click on a download, the paste prompt, or the OpenRouter keys page, and an optional channel tag (ref) from a fixed list. A missing tag is left off. It never includes the words of a decision.

Vercel, which hosts this site, does the counting. When Grill turns it on, PostHog counts the same things too, in a PostHog account that belongs to Grill alone. Neither one sets a cookie or saves anything in your browser, and neither builds a profile of you, records the page, or looks up where you are.

The Pro launch list

If you join the Pro launch list, we keep your email address, and only that, in Resend until Pro launches, and you can unsubscribe with one click.

The details are in the privacy notes. Questions: support@grillyour.ai.
