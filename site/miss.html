Share a miss, not your decision.

When AI got a decision wrong, tell Grill how it went wrong. Grill turns it into a made-up test case, and the judge has to catch it from then on.

What you send

A few choices, one sentence on what would have caught it, and a short example in made-up names and numbers with the same mistake. Not your decision, not your chat, and no real names or numbers. Your assistant can write the card for you: say "draft a miss card".

Cards go to a private queue that only Grill's maintainers read. No IP address, no account, no install ID. Nothing is published as you wrote it. Details: Privacy.

This box has no name, so the browser never sends it. The page checks your card against it and stops you if a real name, number or phrase slipped in.
