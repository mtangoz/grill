Write to us

Grill is made by Hold. You can reach us at support@grillyour.ai. That address is for your account, sign-in, privacy, or deleting your data.

FAQ, terms and privacy

Questions are on the FAQ. The plain terms are on Terms. What Grill keeps is on Privacy.

Delete your data

The free tool has no account. Grill keeps a grill from it only when you send that one.

Grill in chat keeps a record only if you say yes. See, export or delete any record, or all of them, at Your grills.

Deleting your account at /account removes your records, your key and your sign-in.

If you cannot sign in, write to support@grillyour.ai.
