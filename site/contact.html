Contact Grill.

Write to support@grillyour.ai for anything: your account, sign-in, privacy, or deleting your data.

Email

Grill is made by Hold. You can reach us at support@grillyour.ai. That address is for anything: your account, sign-in, privacy, or deleting your data. Your decisions stay private. See Privacy.

See, export or delete any record, or all of them, at grillyour.ai/decisions. Deleting your account at grillyour.ai/account removes your records, your key and your sign-in. Questions: support@grillyour.ai. Who makes Grill is on About.

Bugs and ideas

Bugs and ideas go to GitHub issues. Say what you’d like Grill to do. Don’t paste a real decision. Issues are public. Never paste decision content into a public issue.

Security

Report a vulnerability privately: use Report a vulnerability on this repo's Security tab, or email support@grillyour.ai. Please don't open a public issue. The steps are in SECURITY.md.
