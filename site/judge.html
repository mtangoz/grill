Grill it in your browser.

Hear the strongest case against your plan, then decide for yourself.

Paste a write-up and your OpenRouter key. A model from a different company argues the other side. The verdict is advice. You decide. The key and the text stay on this device and go straight to OpenRouter. Grill stores nothing.

The key stays in this tab. Remember keeps it in this browser only. Forget key deletes it. It is sent only to OpenRouter, as the request's credential.

Other does not name a company, so Grill cannot check that the judge is a different one.

The block is a version 1 grill-record. Copy it or download it. Nothing is stored on a server. Edit the prediction and the confidence before you copy, if the verdict moved you. A decided line is what you chose, in your own words, including any change you made because of a challenge. Add it only after you say it. If this choice replaced an earlier record, add supersedes as that record's date and title, and changed as evidence, goals, context or reweighed, then your few words. A re-grill where nothing changed leaves those lines off. Grill does not invent them, and it does not tell you what to decide.

  


  
Ask your assistant for a link

  
Or paste the write-up above. You can give Claude or Gemini this line:

  
Summarize the decision and give me an Open in Grill link.

  
The link looks like https://grillyour.ai/judge#text=…&from=claude. The write-up sits after the #, so the browser does not send it to Grill. Set from to claude, gemini, chatgpt, grok, or other. Do not put your key in the link.
