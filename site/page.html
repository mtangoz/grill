Privacy

No server, no account, no tracking. Only the write-up you approve leaves. By copy and paste, it goes to the assistant you paste it into. With your own key, it goes only to zero-retention endpoints, with keys blocked and contact details masked: to the judge, and to Jev, a quality check that’s on by default. Grill tells you before each grill, and you can switch Jev off. Grill improves under the same controls, and never sees a real decision. Details are in the privacy notes.
