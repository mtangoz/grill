Before you decide

  1. What do you expect to happen, and by when? Write the prediction you will stand behind.
  2. How sure are you now, as a percent? A band is fine. This is your confidence, not the judge.
  3. What would prove you wrong? Name the one result you will treat as decisive.
```grill-record
version: 1
date: 2026-09-27
title: Take the job in Denver
prediction: I will still want the job after a month in Denver
verdict: shaky
falsifier: spend a long weekend there and do the commute at rush hour
confidence: 80%
review: 2026-10-11
```

How it works

It’s called Grill because a good plan can take a few tough questions.

  1. Tell your AI what you’re deciding, and how sure you are.
  2. It writes a short summary. You check it before anything is shared.
  3. A second AI, from a different company, looks for the weak spots and suggests a quick way to check each one.
  4. You get a simple result: solid, solid if…, shaky, or doesn’t hold up.
  5. Before you decide, you write what you expect, how sure you are, and what would prove you wrong. The record is yours. Paste it back later to look back. Grill does not store it.

Your privacy

  
The free tool has no account and keeps nothing. The reflection at the end of a grill stays in your notes. This page does not store it. Only the short summary you approve is shared, never your notes or files. If you copy and paste, it goes to the AI you paste it into. With the one-click version, it goes only to AI services that don’t store it, plus a quality check that’s on by default (Grill tells you each time, and you can turn it off). Email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers are hidden before anything is sent. And Grill gets better by practicing on made-up examples, never on your decisions. An account is only for a key we manage. A starter allowance needs no card. Bring your own key and there is no account. We see your email, the assistant and judge you pick, and your key’s usage (cost and model, never the text). We count sign-ups, keys issued, first grills, allowances used up and upgrade clicks. We don’t keep what you decide unless you turn on saving copies of reports on that account, and you can delete them. This website counts visits anonymously, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you. The installed tool can optionally send one anonymous usage ping to this site after the first successful grill, if you turn that on at install; off by default; never again; never decision words. If you join the Pro launch list, we keep your email address, and only that, in Resend until Pro launches, and you can unsubscribe with one click. The same count can record that a decision record was shown or copied, that a look-back started or finished, and how many records were pasted. It can also record a click on a download, the paste prompt, or the OpenRouter keys page, and an optional channel tag (ref) from a fixed list. A missing tag is left off. It never includes the words of a decision. The details are in the privacy notes.
