How it works

  1. Say “grill this”Tell the AI you already use what you’re deciding.
  2. A different AI pushes backA model from another company gives a verdict and one cheap test.
  3. You make the callThe verdict never becomes the decision. Grill keeps nothing.

What Grill promises

A different companyThe judge never comes from the company behind your AI, so it won’t share its blind spots. Claude Desktop and Claude Code enforce that in code. The copy-and-paste routes check it and warn you.
Nothing keptThe free tool has no account and keeps none of your decisions. Only the summary you approve is sent.
You decideGrill gives you the case against and a cheap test. The call stays yours.

Every detail of what is sent and counted is in Privacy. Already grilled a decision? Look back at how it turned out.
