# Trademarks

The code is MIT. The name is not part of that license.

"Grill", the pitchfork logo, and grillyour.ai name the official Grill builds and site, made by Grill's maintainers. The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the code. It gives no rights to the name or the logo.

## Why

People trust Grill with decisions that matter because they can check what the official build does with their words. A fork under the same name could change what leaves their machine, and the people using it couldn't tell.

## You can

- Fork it, change it, ship it and sell it, under the MIT license.
- Say your project is "based on Grill", "a fork of Grill", or "works with Grill".
- Use the name to talk about Grill truthfully: in a review, a comparison, a tutorial or a news story.
- Pass on an unmodified official release under its name, for example in a mirror or a package manager.

## Please don't

- Name a fork or a product "Grill", or anything close enough to be confused with it.
- Use the logo on a build that isn't an unmodified official release.
- List a changed build in a store, registry or directory under the Grill name.
- Suggest that Grill's maintainers made, checked or endorse something they didn't.
