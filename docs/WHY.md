# Why not just ask your own assistant?

It helped you think it through, so its critique shares your blind spots. A judge from another company doesn't. Claude Desktop and Claude Code enforce that in code, and tell you if a run lands on your own company anyway. The copy-and-paste routes check it and warn you.

The judge must argue your side before it attacks, quote the words it targets, and give every challenge a test that would settle it. A verdict of "solid" with no challenges is a real answer; it's told a made-up objection is worse than none.

The checks on a single grill, and how Grill gets better without reading a real decision, are in [LEARNING.md](../LEARNING.md).
