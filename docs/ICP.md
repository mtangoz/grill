# Early-adopter ICP

Approved by Tony on 1 November 2026. This file names who early outreach is for, and the rules that outreach follows. It sits under [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md). If the two disagree, PRINCIPLES.md wins. A real user is still a person who ran a grill.

Grill's long-term position is a cross-provider second opinion. It works with any AI assistant or agent. The judge is a model from a different company than the one that helped with the decision. The person still decides.

ChatGPT and Claude are examples of surfaces people use today.

## Primary ICP

People who already make real decisions with an AI assistant or agent, from any provider, and who say in public that they distrust a same-model self-check.

That shows up among founders, product managers, indie builders, and operations leads. What they share is the job and the distrust. The provider they type into is incidental.

What matches them is a verdict from a different company, plus one cheap test, with the call left in their hands. When a post is asking for a person to check the work, the reply offers that cheap test and leaves the decision with them.

## Secondary ICP

One secondary audience: people who build agents or automations and grill many small plans in a row. That is the bulk path. The speed rule in [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md) already applies there: a quick or bulk grill gets a verdict with no required question in front of it.

They stay secondary unless a pool-size check and early conversion say the primary pool is too thin to cover the first week's leads. A swap of who the public copy speaks to waits for the re-review below, and needs Tony.

## Where to reach them

They are already posting about a real decision, or about an assistant checking its own answer.

- X is the first place. The public account is [@AskGrill](https://x.com/AskGrill). Its bio says the account is an AI and points at Tony. Reddit and Hacker News are the second places, and replies there stay on threads that are already public.
- Reply on a post that is already public, or answer someone who tagged or messaged that account. A public reply can be a verdict on what they already said in public. A direct message does not carry a decision or a verdict.
- The outreach call to action is [https://grillyour.ai/start/?ref=askgrill](https://grillyour.ai/start/?ref=askgrill) plus a single-use invite code. That is the link and the code, not a decision sent in a direct message.
- Someone who has shown interest can use Grill on whatever assistant or agent they already have, including the current hosted connectors. Those connectors are invite-only. The code is redeemed at `/redeem`, then the setup guide is `/start`. The demo page shows the format.

## Approved rules

These were approved with the ICP on 1 November 2026.

**First value on a public post is a verdict.** A public reply can open with a verdict on something they already said in public, plus one cheap test. A direct message does not. The call to action in outreach is `/start?ref=askgrill` plus a single-use invite code. A later message can also point at the paste path and the demo page. The demo shows format only. What each path keeps is in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). This file adds no promise.

**An invite only after interest, and Tony names each invitee.** The hosted connector stays invite-only. Ask for an invite after the person has replied with interest. Tony names each person. The thing they receive is the `/start` link and one invite code, not a decision in a direct message. Tony also sets the daily maximum for public verdicts and invite decisions. Past that maximum, the reply still points at the paste path and the demo. A sequence to the same person can run for about 14 days. The invite code is the last step, and only after that reply.

**Stop after 100 distinct leads** when any of these is true: replies are under 5% of those leads, complaints are over 1% of those leads, or anyone blocks the account. One distinct lead is one person who received at least one outbound direct message. Later messages to the same person stay one lead. Rewrite the outreach before sending more.

Before going past the first 100 leads, count distinct authors over 7 days for primary-style threads and for agent or automation threads. Hand-classify about 20 authors from the primary set by bio and role, as a decision-maker or a spectator. Log those counts with the first batch. If decision-makers are a small share of that sample, or the primary threads cannot cover the first week's leads, narrow the copy or raise the secondary audience's weight, then re-review.

**A person approves replies until platform checks pass.** A person approves each public reply and each direct message until the platform's written rules allow automation and a delivery check has passed. The first two weeks are daily batches Tony approves. A delivery check measures whether a message arrives. The rate at which strangers accept a cold message comes from the first real batch. Public replies stay on posts that are already public. A direct message carries the `/start` link and an invite code. It does not carry a decision or a verdict.

**Tag replies by topic, and re-review this ICP** when a cohort reaches 10 evidenced users or 100 distinct leads, whichever comes first. Tag what they asked to grill: a work decision, an agent or bulk plan, both, or unclear. Join the tag to the evidenced first grill as a note of topic only: the topic tag, and nothing from the decision or the message. Tony gets one page: conversion per cohort, and a recommendation to keep or swap the primary audience. Swapping the public copy needs Tony.

**Share links are proof only.** A share link shows a verdict and one challenge. Use it as proof in a later message. Directory listings, open sign-up, and an invite bonus stay off this path until their own triggers in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), and until Tony says so.

## Review triggers

Bring this file back when any of these happen. Change it when the evidence changes, and say what changed. A swap of the primary audience is grilled before the public copy changes.

- The public account is blocked. Outreach continues as public replies on posts that are already public.
- The main product path fails to activate. Fix that path before more outreach.
- A cohort reaches 10 evidenced users, or 100 distinct leads. Write the one-page re-review. If it favors the secondary audience, swap the primary and grill that version before changing public copy.
- 100 distinct leads, and replies are under 5%, complaints are over 1%, or there is any block. Stop and rewrite.
- The paste path starts to require an account. The cold path becomes a product fix, recorded here.
- 28 days after the first approved direct-message batch. The denominator is distinct leads who received at least one outbound direct message. Success is at least 3 evidenced outcomes in that window: a completed paste grill, a hosted invite Tony named that has at least one hosted grill, or an outreach reply that confirms the person ran a grill. Failure is fewer than 1 evidenced outcome after 100 distinct leads, or a reply rate under 5% of those 100 leads.

Count those outcomes the way [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md) counts a real user. A download, a launch-list sign-up, and a view of a share link stay off that count.
