# Competitors

Notes from looking at other products that touch the same job. This is not a ranking, and it is not a claim about users. The daily growth work reviews docs/ROADMAP.md before it asks what to build. A weekly scan updates docs/ROADMAP.md and docs/COMPETITORS.md together. New ideas get a consider-when line in the roadmap. A scan adds a date and what changed here.

## Cruxes

- Site: [cruxes.io](https://cruxes.io)
- What it does: finds the one question that would change a decision.
- Words: quotes the user's text.
- Shape: team and agent harnesses with a decision owner.
- Where it runs: Claude Code, Claude and Google Docs.
- Price: 15 free, then $29 a month.

### What we learned

The one question is worth showing, after the verdict, in the person's own words, and it has to stay short. A shared session needs a named decision owner. Google Docs and agent harnesses are places people already decide. Fifteen free checks, then $29 a month, is evidence that some people pay for this job. Grill's own price is a separate decision, and Pro is not live.

### Threat level: medium

Cruxes is close on the one question, and it is already in team sessions, agent harnesses, and Google Docs. Grill's difference is a verdict from a different company, plus one cheap test. The overlap is the question, not the whole product.
