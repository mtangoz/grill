# Grill brand

## Voice

Simple, confident, open. Less is more.

- Say what happened, give one next step, then stop.
- The person makes the call. We give a view, not an order.
- Be honest about what we don't know, and don't apologise for having an opinion.
- Use short sentences and everyday words. If a line can go, cut it.
- Invite a reply instead of saying "no pressure".

## Who we speak to

People already making real decisions with AI who distrust an AI checking its own work. They include founders, PMs, indie builders and ops leads. They want a verdict from an AI company different from the one that helped them, one cheap test, and control of the decision. See [ICP.md](ICP.md) for the approved audience and outreach rules.

Use the existing pitchfork mark beside “Grill.” Keep it small; never make the logo carry the message. Use approved existing logo assets, not emoji or a new drawing. The current mark is a light pitchfork on a dark square.

Type: system sans, matching `site/page.html`; Arial/Helvetica fallback in email. Monospace for codes and technical values only. Sentence case, left alignment, generous space. No gradients, shadows, hero illustrations or decorative panels. Preserve readable contrast in dark mode.

## Words

**Use:** decision, second opinion, different AI company, short verdict, one cheap test, try, reply, “You make the call.”

**Avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, unlock, seamless, supercharge, guaranteed, right answer, “just checking in,” “stay tuned,” “contact an administrator.” No em dashes. Spell out technical terms unless the step requires them.

Do not promise certainty, unshipped features, directory availability or universal setup times. Never request calls. Keep invitations optional; open sign-up does not require a code. Keep user decisions out of outreach DMs, subjects and reminders.

## Notifications

Sender name: **Grill**. One purpose, at most two short editorial lines, one optional action. Code emails show the code instead of a button. Security and billing details stay intact in a small details block. Use `support@grillyour.ai` as a real help route; do not imply a noreply address accepts replies.

## Approved email copy

- Sign-in code. Subject: Your Grill code. Body: "{{otp_code}}" then "Enter this in Grill to sign in. Don't share it with anyone. If you didn't ask for it, you can ignore this email."
- Invite. Subject: Try Grill on your next decision. Body: "Before you act on what your AI says, get a second opinion. Grill asks an AI from a different company and gives you a short verdict and one cheap test. You make the call." Button: Connect Grill.
- New device. Subject: New sign-in to Grill. Body: "Your account was just signed in on a new device. If that was you, there's nothing to do. If not, sign that device out below." Button: Sign out that device.

## Privacy

When explaining decisions or asking for feedback, use this approved wording unchanged and link to https://grillyour.ai/terms:

> Grill only uses your decisions to give you a verdict and your later look-backs. We don't read them, share them or use them for anything else, so we can't learn what's working unless you tell us.

Do not say hosted Grill sees or stores nothing. Do not paste the full paragraph into sign-in-code emails; a Privacy link is enough there. Preserve the actual model/timing/privacy run note in verdict output. Never invent it from memory.

## Before → after

- “Your sign in link” → **“Sign in to Grill.”** Body: “Your sign-in link expires in {{ttl_minutes}} minutes. Didn’t request it? Ignore this email.” Action: **Sign in**.
- “This is an automatic notification to inform you that the password … has been changed” → **“Your account password was changed. Not you? Contact support@grillyour.ai.”**
- A long tester invitation with setup steps and a call request → **“An AI from a different company gives you a short verdict and one cheap test. You make the call.”** Action: **Connect Grill**.

A brand edit never changes auth methods, privacy promises, offers, notification delivery or publishing authority. Keep those approvals separate.
